1. The system, end to end

A drone watches the battlefield. Deterministic functions extract features (distance to friendlies, target hardness, weather, airspace). Six decision sub-trees run in sequence and produce a draft 9-line CAS brief. The JTAC reviews. The Ground Force Commander approves. Then it goes to the aircrew. Nothing is generated by an LLM. Nothing fires automatically.

Doctrinal frame: our drone is a JFO, not a JTAC
A Joint Fires Observer feeds targeting data and marks targets but cannot independently terminate a CAS attack. So the system's output is a draft brief for a JTAC, not a strike order. This is the legal posture that lets the system exist.
Source: JFO ATTP ¶1-3, ¶1-9
Why deterministic instead of an LLM
Every output traces to a specific YAML rule and a specific manual paragraph. Hallucinations are impossible because nothing is generated. Operators trust it because it matches their mental model. Novel scenarios fail safely by routing to "JTAC review required."

Key facts
  • Self-defense bypasses NSL and ROE category checks, but PID + airspace + danger close are preserved.
  • NSL Cat I = hospitals, schools, religious sites, diplomatic missions, refugee camps, POW camps, dams whose breach floods civilians.

  • CNN confidence threshold 0.85 is a starting value — tunable with Collen.

Key facts
  • JDAM family (GBU-31/32/38) requires CAT I (<10 m) coordinates — drone alone produces CAT III, so JDAMs are eliminated unless coordinates are mensurated.

  • If a Cat I no-strike site is inside the weapon's RED, the weapon is eliminated. No initials override that.
  • If friendlies are inside RED, the weapon flags DANGER CLOSE — not blocked, but requires GFC initials and Type 1 control if viable.

Key facts
  • Type 1 is doctrinally prohibited for GPS-guided weapons (JDAMs). When the munition tree picks a JDAM, control type must be Type 2.

  • Type 2 is the canonical default when the drone is the JTAC's only target sight (JP 3-09.3 III-45).
  • Type 3 ("Cleared to Engage") needs explicit authority from the supported commander and an explicit attack envelope: time window, geographic boundary, FAH, target set.

Key facts
  • IR pointers MARK, they don't GUIDE. "Unlike LASER designators, IR pointers cannot be used to guide or improve the accuracy of aircraft ordnance" (JFO ATTP ¶1-65).
  • Whenever laser is in use, the system passes Laser-to-Target Line (LTL) and Final Attack Heading (FAH) cone in remarks. FAH cone = LTL+10° to LTL+60° clockwise (10° safety zone, 60° outer acquisition basket).
  • Always plan a redundant mark. Preferred combos: laser+smoke, IR pointer+IR strobe, smoke+talk-on.
  • Talk-on uses cardinal/sub-cardinal directions only — never clock positions (no shared reference frame between JTAC and aircrew).
Source: JP 3-09.3 III-77 to III-81; JFO ATTP ¶1-63 to ¶1-158

Tree 5 — IP / egress geometry

Purpose: Choose the best Initial Point and egress direction. Pure geometry, not a tree — implemented as a scoring function over pre-loaded named IPs.

Scoring function (sketch)
  • Penalty: run-in flies over friendlies, intersects active threat ring (MEZ), or violates airspace measure.
  • Bonus: terrain masking on the run-in, pilot familiarity (named IP in fragged ATO), egress away from threats and toward FARP/recovery.
  • Note: Lines 1–3 (IP, heading, distance from IP) describe the aircraft route. The JTAC controlling the strike picks those from their pre-loaded named IPs — our system outputs Lines 4–9 + Remarks and recommends an IP/egress to the JTAC.
Source: JP 3-09.3 V-9 (9-line format); JSEAD 2015

Glossary

Every acronym used in the diagrams above. A non-JTAC viewer should never have to leave this page.

9-line
The standardized 9-line briefing format a JTAC reads to attack aircraft. The system's primary output.
AGM-114
Hellfire missile. Fired from helicopters and drones; laser-guided variants most common. RED 110-130 m.
AGM-65
Maverick missile. IIR-seeker variants are autonomous after launch; preferred for movers.
BOC / BOT
Bomb on Coordinate / Bomb on Target. Weapon aimed at GPS coords vs at the target the aircraft sees.
CAS
Close Air Support — aircraft attacking enemies near friendly troops. The whole problem we're solving.
CAT I/II/III
Target Location Error categories. CAT I < ~10 m (GPS-bomb-grade). CAT III ~30 m (drone alone).
CBR
Chemical, Biological, Radiological — hazards that escalate CDE to Level 5.
CDE
Collateral Damage Estimation. 5-level escalation methodology for predicting civilian harm.
CER
Collateral Effects Radius. The danger circle radius for a planned strike.
CHA
Collateral Hazard Area. The danger circle around a planned strike.
CJCSI
Chairman of the Joint Chiefs of Staff Instruction. Authoritative DoD-level guidance.
CNN
Convolutional Neural Network — the drone's onboard target classifier.
Cleared Hot
"Drop the weapon now." Type 1 / Type 2.
Cleared To Engage
Multi-attack version of Cleared Hot. Type 3 only.
CRP
Common Reference Point — landmark both JTAC and aircrew can identify.
Danger Close
Friendlies inside the weapon's RED standing distance. Adds GFC-initials procedure; doesn't prohibit.
DShK
Soviet 12.7 mm heavy machine gun. Common technical-mounted weapon.
FAC(A)
Forward Air Controller (Airborne) — pilot qualified to do JTAC's job from the air.
FAH
Final Attack Heading — magnetic heading the aircraft must be on when releasing the weapon.
FARP
Forward Arming and Refueling Point. Where helicopters/aircraft return between sorties.
FMV
Full-Motion Video — what the drone produces.
GBU-12
500 lb laser-guided bomb (Paveway II). The CAS workhorse. RED 275 m.
GBU-31/32/38
JDAM family. GPS-guided bombs (2000/1000/500 lb). Need CAT I coordinates.
GBU-39
Small Diameter Bomb (250 lb GPS-guided). Smaller frag, used near civilians. RED 205 m.
GFC
Ground Force Commander — senior on-scene commander; final approver above the JTAC.
GPS
Global Positioning System — satellite-based location. Drives JDAM guidance.
GTL
Gun-Target Line — the imaginary line from a friendly artillery piece to its target. Aircraft must deconflict.
IIR
Imaging Infrared — a thermal seeker (used by Maverick). Autonomous after launch.
IP
Initial Point — named geographic point the aircraft flies from toward the target. Line 1.
IR pointer
Infrared laser pointer — visible only through NVGs. Marks targets at night. Cannot guide weapons.
JDAM
Joint Direct Attack Munition — GPS-guided bomb (GBU-31/32/38).
JFIRE
Multi-service pocket reference. Source of RED tables 81-87.
JFO
Joint Fires Observer — less-certified observer who feeds targeting data. Doctrinally, our drone+system is closest to a JFO.
JP 3-09.3
Joint Publication on Close Air Support (2014). The bible. Most-cited document in our tree.
JTAC
Joint Terminal Attack Controller — the certified ground operator who clears aircraft to drop bombs.
LGB / LGW
Laser-Guided Bomb / Weapon. Needs a laser spot on the target.
LST
Laser Spot Tracker — aircraft sensor that locks onto a laser spot.
LTL
Laser-to-Target Line — magnetic bearing from designator to target. Drives the FAH cone.
MANPADS
Man-Portable Air Defense System — shoulder-fired anti-aircraft missiles.
MEZ
Missile Engagement Zone — airspace dominated by enemy surface-to-air missiles.
MGRS
Military Grid Reference System — alphanumeric grid format for target location.
MSL / AGL
Mean Sea Level / Above Ground Level — altitude reference points.
NFA / RFA
No-Fire Area / Restricted Fire Area — airspace overlays that gate weapons release.
NSL
No-Strike List — protected sites (hospitals, schools, mosques). Hard block.
NVG / NVD
Night Vision Goggles / Device. IR pointers visible only through these.
PID
Positive Identification — reasonable certainty target is legitimate. Required before any engagement.
Pᵢ
Probability of incapacitation. RED is published at 0.1% Pᵢ (1-in-1000 risk to a standing soldier).
PRF
Pulse Repetition Frequency — the unique blink pattern of a laser. 4-digit code (e.g., 1688).
PSS-SOF
Precision Strike Suite — mensuration tool that produces CAT I coordinates.
RED
Risk Estimate Distance — how far friendlies should be from impact, by weapon type. Drives danger-close threshold.
ROE
Rules of Engagement — legal/policy rules for using force. Theater-specific.
ROVER / VDL
Video Downlink — JTAC's tablet that shows drone or aircraft sensor feed.
SCAR
Strike Coordination And Reconnaissance — finding targets and directing strikes (no friendlies nearby).
