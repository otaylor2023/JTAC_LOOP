JTAC Decision Tree — Feedback Review

National security hackathon project: deterministic 9-line generator from drone video + friendly positions. Below are 10 mock scenarios + 8 open doctrinal questions. Your job: tell us where the system is wrong, right, or close-but-not-quite, and answer the open questions.

How to read each scenario

  1. Situation — the inputs the system would receive (target, friendlies, weather, etc.).
  2. System recommendation — what the deterministic tree outputs (top pick + alternatives + reasoning).
  3. Per-line grading — thumbs up / down / unsure for each line, plus a comment box.
  4. Overall grade — would you actually transmit this 9-line, alter it, or deny it?
  5. What we want from you — the specific judgment calls flagged for your input.

All feedback saves to your browser as you go. When you're done, hit "Export" or "Copy to clipboard."
